Research

Research

Cryptography and computing systems designed for security, privacy, and trustworthy execution.

Post-Quantum Cryptography

I study the post-quantum transition as a systems problem, including standards-aligned implementations, interoperability, cryptographic agility, performance, and reproducible validation.

Privacy-Enhancing Technologies

My work includes fully homomorphic encryption, secure computation, and system-level approaches to protecting sensitive data during computation.

Confidential Intelligence

Confidential Intelligence asks what forms of computation we would design if confidentiality were treated as a first-class design principle from the outset.

Secure AI and Hardware Security

I study secure intelligent systems, side-channel exposure, fault injection, architectural protection, and high-performance cryptographic hardware and software.
