Ro Cammarota

Associate Professor of Computer Science

My research spans cryptography, privacy-enhancing technologies, post-quantum security, secure AI, computer systems, architecture, and hardware security. I am particularly interested in making security and privacy guarantees foundational to modern computing.

I direct the Confidential Intelligence Lab at UC Irvine.

Research

Post-Quantum Cryptography

High-assurance implementations, interoperability, cryptographic agility, systems efficiency, and reproducible evaluation.

Privacy-Enhancing Technologies

Fully homomorphic encryption, secure computation, and systems for protecting sensitive information during computation.

Confidential Intelligence

Co-designing computational paradigms, cryptography, software, architectures, and hardware around confidentiality.

Secure AI and Hardware Security

Side channels, fault resilience, secure accelerators, and trustworthy architectures.
