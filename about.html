About

Ro Cammarota

Associate Professor of Computer Science
University of California, Irvine

My work spans cryptography, computer systems, secure AI, and hardware security, with a longstanding emphasis on translating foundational security research into practical computing systems.

Before joining UC Irvine, I held senior research and technology leadership roles in industry, including at Intel, where I founded and led the Encrypted Computing program and served as Principal Investigator for the DARPA DPRIVE program.

I lead the development of the ISO/IEC 28033 standard series for Fully Homomorphic Encryption.

My research mission is to make security and privacy guarantees foundational to modern computing.

I direct the Confidential Intelligence Lab at UC Irvine.
